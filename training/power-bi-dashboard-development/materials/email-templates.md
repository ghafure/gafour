# Training Email and Communication Templates

## Pre-work email

Subject: Pre-work for Power BI Dashboard Development Training

Hello,

You are registered for Power BI Dashboard Development training. Before the session, please complete the following:

1. Install Power BI Desktop.
2. Confirm you can open Power BI Desktop.
3. Download the training folder and locate `data/sales_sample.csv`.
4. Bring one example of a dashboard or report you use today.
5. Think of one KPI that is frequently debated in your team.

During the course, you will build a sales performance dashboard and complete a short capstone presentation.

Thank you.

## Day 1 follow-up email

Subject: Day 1 recap and preparation for modeling

Hello,

Today we covered dashboard requirements, KPI definitions, wireframing, and Power Query data preparation. Before the next session, please review your dashboard brief and KPI catalog. Confirm that your sample data loads correctly and that the key fields have correct data types.

Tomorrow we will focus on semantic models, relationships, and DAX measures.

## Day 2 follow-up email

Subject: Day 2 recap and capstone preparation

Hello,

Today we covered semantic modeling, DAX measures, report design, accessibility, and performance. Before the final session, please make sure you have:

- A working model.
- At least five explicit measures.
- A draft Executive Summary page.
- Notes about validation issues or design questions.

Tomorrow we will cover publishing, governance, QA, and capstone presentations.

## Post-training email

Subject: Power BI Dashboard Development training resources

Hello,

Thank you for participating in Power BI Dashboard Development training. Your next step is to apply the same workflow to a real reporting need:

1. Start with the dashboard brief.
2. Confirm KPI definitions with stakeholders.
3. Build a model before designing pages.
4. Validate measures before publishing.
5. Document refresh, ownership, permissions, and support.

Recommended resources in the training folder:

- `learner-guide.md`
- `materials/quick-reference.md`
- `templates/dashboard-brief.md`
- `templates/kpi-catalog.md`
- `templates/dashboard-qa-checklist.md`
- `templates/dax-measures.md`

Thank you.
