# Lab 01: Requirements and Wireframe

## Goal

Create a clear dashboard brief, KPI catalog, and first-page wireframe before opening Power BI Desktop.

## Scenario

The sales leadership team wants a dashboard to monitor revenue performance, target attainment, product mix, and returns across regions and salespeople.

## Tasks

1. Open `templates/dashboard-brief.md`.
2. Define the primary audience and the decisions they need to make.
3. Write at least five stakeholder discovery questions.
4. Open `templates/kpi-catalog.md`.
5. Define Revenue, Orders, Average Order Value, Target Attainment %, and Return Rate.
6. Sketch a landing page with:
   - One page title.
   - Four KPI cards.
   - One trend visual.
   - One comparison visual.
   - Two to four slicers.
   - One insight or alert area.
7. Write three acceptance criteria.

## Deliverables

- Completed dashboard brief.
- Completed KPI catalog.
- Wireframe image or text sketch.

## Success criteria

- The audience is specific.
- KPIs include formulas and validation sources.
- The wireframe supports decisions rather than showing every possible field.
